About Me Profile

I am a final-year Computer Science student at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science and Technology (QUEST), Nawabshah, Pakistan, currently working on final year project at the Computer Vision Lab under the supervision of Dr. Ali Asghar Chandio, focusing on 3D monocular vision and multi-view 3D object reconstruction.

Previously, I completed a research-oriented training program at the Applied AI Lab, WorldQuant University (WQU), working on Edge AI pipelines, generative models for medical imaging, and model deployment for resource-constrained devices. I also completed internships at Sindh Bank Limited (IT Division) and Mashreq Bank as a remote software developer, and I am a Senior Microsoft Student Ambassador and Aspire Leaders Program (Harvard / Aspire Institute) alum.

My academic work centers on deep learning, computer vision with a particular interest in medical imaging applications. I am the co-author of a peer-reviewed submission on explainable medical image segmentation, currently under review.
